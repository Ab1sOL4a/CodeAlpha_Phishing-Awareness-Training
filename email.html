How to Recognize a Phishing Email

Phishing emails are designed to look legitimate. Learning to identify small warning signs can help prevent you from falling for an attack.

Example: Suspicious Email

From: security@paypa1-support.com

Subject: URGENT! Your account will be suspended

Hello Customer,

We detected unusual activity on your account. Your account will be permanently suspended within 24 hours unless you verify your information.

Click here to verify your account

Thank you,
PayPal Security Team

What Should You Notice?

1. Suspicious Sender Address

The address uses paypa1-support.com instead of an official PayPal domain. Notice the use of the number "1" instead of the letter "l".

2. Urgent Language

The message creates pressure by saying the account will be suspended within 24 hours.

3. Suspicious Link

The email asks the user to click a link and verify sensitive account information.

4. Generic Greeting

The email says "Hello Customer" instead of using the recipient's name.

What Should You Do?

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Do not provide passwords or OTPs through email links.
  • Visit the company's official website directly.
  • Report suspicious messages to your organization.
