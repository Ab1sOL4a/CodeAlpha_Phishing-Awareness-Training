What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person, company, or service in order to trick someone into revealing sensitive information.

How Does Phishing Work?

Phishing attacks usually rely on deception and social engineering. The attacker may send an email, text message, or create a fake website that looks legitimate.

The goal may be to steal information such as:

  • Passwords
  • Bank or payment information
  • Login credentials
  • Personal information
  • One-time passwords (OTP)

Example

Imagine receiving an email claiming that your bank account will be suspended unless you verify your account immediately.

The message contains a link that takes you to a website that looks exactly like your bank's website.

If you enter your username and password, the attacker may receive the information.

Common Warning Signs

  • Unexpected messages or emails
  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Suspicious links
  • Requests for passwords or sensitive information
  • Unusual sender addresses
  • Spelling or grammar mistakes
