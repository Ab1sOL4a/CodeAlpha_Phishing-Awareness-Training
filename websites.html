Think Before You Click

How to Spot a Fake Website

Attackers can create websites that look almost identical to legitimate services. Before entering sensitive information, always inspect the website carefully.

What Should You Check?

1. Check the Website Address

Look carefully at the domain name. Attackers may use misspelled words, extra characters, or misleading subdomains to imitate legitimate websites.

2. Don't Trust HTTPS Alone

HTTPS encrypts the connection between your browser and the website, but it does not automatically mean that the website itself is legitimate.

3. Look for Unexpected Login Pages

Be careful if you arrive at a login page after clicking an unexpected email or text message.

4. Check for Strange Spelling or Design

Poor grammar, unusual logos, broken images, or inconsistent design can sometimes indicate a fraudulent website.

Example of a Suspicious Address

https://paypa1-login.example.com

PayPal

Sign in to continue

The page may look convincing, but the important clue is the website address. Always inspect the actual domain before entering your credentials.

Safer Browsing Habits

  • Type important website addresses yourself or use a trusted bookmark.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information after clicking unexpected links.
  • Check the domain carefully before logging in.
  • Use a password manager where appropriate; it can help distinguish the expected website domain.
  • If something feels unusual, stop and verify the website through an independent source.
